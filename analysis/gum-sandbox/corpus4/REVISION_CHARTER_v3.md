# CORPUS4 — The Electroweak + Strong Fold: Charter (v3)

**What this is.** A proposed revision of the corpus3 editions producing
**corpus4**: every corpus3 document re-issued as a new edition with the
**electroweak sector** (from the synthesis line: `synthesis/
GUM-Omega-Synthesis-v4.4-EW.md` §8, carried verbatim through v4.5-SS) and
the **expanded strong sector** (the Tier-8/9/10 campaign phases: findings
F-T8-S1…S4, F-T9-T1…T4, F-T10-U1…U3; adjudications in
`tier8-assessment/`, `tier9-glueball/`, `tier10-junction/`) folded in.
This corpus4 edition set was **commissioned by the corpus's owner**
(session goal, 2026-08-16); the synthesis-line EW content therefore
enters as adopted-at-printed-grade, while campaign-side strong-sector
items retain their offer/proposal labels exactly as adjudicated.  Every
change is within-model; nothing here bears on nature.

**Revision rules (binding on every corpus4 document; inherits the
corpus3 charter v2, with these updates):**
1. **S-30 compliance**: corpus3's documents are never rewritten in
   place — corpus4 contains NEW editions; the corpus3 files stand as
   dated records.  Unlisted corpus3 files are carried forward by
   reference, unchanged.
2. **Core documents get new editions**: Omega paper **v5.0-ext**; Course
   **v4-ext**; Primer **v4-ext**; Teachers' Edition **v4-ext**; Session
   Map **v4.0-ext**; Watch-Mode memo **v4-ext**; External Replication
   Record **v3.0**; README.manifest **v6.0-ext**.  The payload files
   T6_FOLD_PAYLOAD.md / T7_FOLD_PAYLOAD.md are carried verbatim with an
   addendum header pointing to the new payload; FOLD_RECORD.md is
   extended additively.
3. **Marker discipline**: every change carries a leading marker —
   **⟦EW⟧** for synthesis-line electroweak content (citing
   `synthesis/GUM-Omega-Synthesis-v4.5-SS.md` §8 and its flags/
   cross-locks/closures), **⟦T8⟧/⟦T9⟧/⟦T10⟧** for campaign-phase content
   (citing finding tags + artifact paths).  Pre-existing text — including
   every earlier ⟦rev⟧/⟦T6⟧/⟦T7⟧ block — is never deleted or reworded;
   corpus4 editions are the corpus3 text plus additions (sole exceptions:
   version headers/titles and explicitly-marked supersession notes,
   which quote what they supersede).  **Every document's length must
   strictly grow.**
4. **Grades never rise by replication** (retained): ⟦T9⟧/⟦T10⟧
   annotations attach at their adjudicated classes; every
   hadron-mass-confronting number is copied at **V.F strength only**
   ("dimensionally secure / structurally plausible / quantitatively
   unclaimed") under seal q-θ; ⟦EW⟧ content carries the grades printed
   in the synthesis line ([DF | flag]-conditional etc.) — no grade may
   exceed its source.
5. **Offers vs adoptions**: the EW sector is adopted (owner-
   commissioned) at printed grade; campaign-side strong-sector
   annotations (the Tier-9 two-sector note, F-Q8, the Tier-10
   trichotomy/K-1 affixation, census cross-references) enter as adopted-
   by-commission but each box retains its origin label ("campaign
   proposal, adopted this edition") so the provenance is auditable.
6. **Scope honesty**: the X(2370) and STAR junction results are [IM]
   anchors carried with their live rivals (mixing-fraction unknowns;
   CGC-saturation / neutron-skin / strangeness counter-explanations)
   wherever cited; the forbidden sentences ("predicted", "confirms",
   "refutes") remain unavailable in every document; the plain-string
   theorem, the convention split, and every honest negative are carried
   as printed.
7. **Payload authority**: the canonical statement of every foldable
   result is **`EW_SS_FOLD_PAYLOAD.md`** (this directory); document
   editors must not restate numbers or theorem texts from memory — copy
   them from the payload (which itself points to verbatim source
   blocks).  T6/T7 payloads remain authoritative for their own earlier
   blocks.
8. **Verification gates (per document, adjudicated in FOLD_RECORD)**:
   (a) diff vs corpus3 base shows deletions ONLY in version headers/
   supersession notes; (b) line count strictly grows; (c) marker counts
   reported; (d) payload spot-checks verbatim; (e) register scan (no
   forbidden sentences, V.F language on masses).
