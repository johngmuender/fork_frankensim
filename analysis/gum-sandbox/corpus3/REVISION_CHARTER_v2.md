# CORPUS3 — The Tier-6 Fold: Charter

**What this is.** A proposed revision of the corpus2 editions produced by
the external replication campaign's Tier-6 (foundations) phase — the
findings F-T6-1…6, the executed workstreams L1/L2/L3/L5/L5′/L7a/L7b, and
the five proposed annotations of TIER6_ADJUDICATION.md — folded into the
corpus's own documents in its own epistemic register. It is the
**replicators' proposal, not the authors' edition; adoption is theirs.**
Every change is within-model; nothing here bears on nature.

**Revision rules (binding on every corpus3 document; inherits the
corpus2 charter, with these updates):**
1. **S-30 compliance**: corpus2's documents are never rewritten in
   place — corpus3 contains NEW editions (the v4/v3-ext series); the
   corpus2 files stand as dated records. Unlisted corpus2 files are
   carried forward by reference, unchanged.
2. **Core documents get new editions**: Omega paper v4.0-ext; Course
   v3-ext; Primer v3-ext; Teachers' Edition v3-ext; Session Map
   v3.0-ext; Watch-Mode memo v3-ext; External Replication Record v2.0;
   README.manifest v5.0-ext.
3. **Marker discipline**: every Tier-6 change carries a leading **⟦T6⟧**
   marker (distinct from corpus2's ⟦rev⟧, which is preserved verbatim
   wherever it already appears); every ⟦T6⟧ block cites its finding
   ([F-T6-…]) and reproducing artifact path
   (tier6-foundations/…). Pre-existing text is never deleted or
   reworded — v4/v3 editions are the corpus2 text plus ⟦T6⟧ additions
   (the sole exceptions: version headers/titles and explicitly-marked
   supersession notes, which quote what they supersede).
4. **Grades never rise by replication** (corpus2 rule 4 retained):
   ⟦T6⟧ annotations attach at [ext]/[DW, testbed-grade] class;
   discharged debts regrade only in the "conditional-on-named-
   assumption" direction (e.g. Lock 2: GAP-minor →
   conservation-lemma-conditional).
5. **Offers, not adoptions**: the five proposed annotations enter as
   ⟦T6⟧ boxes explicitly labeled proposals; the corpus's authors may
   strike any of them without breaking the edition.
6. **Scope honesty**: the L7 exclusion results carry their
   configuration-specific/testbed-grade labels and the non-perturbing-
   screen premise everywhere they are cited; the L5′ G4 result is
   always reported as FAIL-AT-d25/null-approached, never as a pass.
7. **Payload authority**: the canonical statement of every foldable
   result is `T6_FOLD_PAYLOAD.md` (this directory); document editors
   must not restate numbers from memory — copy them from the payload.
