# PART A — SECTOR AUDIT: THE LOAD-BEARING STORY NOBODY HAS AUDITED

## A.1 The situation

Five campaigns have leaned on one sentence more than any other: **the ground state does not gravitate; departures do** (Gibbs–Duhem, §VI.D). It dissolved the 10¹²⁰ catastrophe structurally; it killed candidate channels in WS-D and WS-G (c6, c7); it earned the one licensed convergence sentence with conformal gravity; it fences the w(z) story every time a campaign walks past it. **And the story itself has never been run at campaign grade.** The corpus's dark-energy account — *the observed Λ-class density is the not-yet-relaxed residual of the vacuum's approach to its ground state* — is a **kinetic claim**, and kinetic claims have derivable consequences: a relaxation law implies an **equation-of-state history w(z)**, and w(z) is precisely what a new generation of surveys ⬛ (DESI-class BAO programs; Euclid; Roman) is now measuring — with current releases showing **contested hints of evolving dark energy** ⬛-rider (thawing-quadrant preferences at few-σ, systematics debates live). The archive's last major observational face is therefore simultaneously its most exposed assumption, its best chance at a genuinely **predictive** product, and — because the data already whisper — its **worst fitting hazard.** This campaign is designed around that triple fact.

## A.2 The five findings

**(1) The sector can produce the archive's first *earned* stake.** Five campaigns declined stakes for five filed reasons — no derivable statistic, credit-farming, category errors. Here, at last, a derivable statistic exists: **the class of w(z)** — the sign of (1+w) today, the direction of its evolution, the asymptote — follows from the relaxation kinetics *if the corpus fixes the kinetics*, with exactly **one calibration in** (the residual's present magnitude, absorbed under α-policy V.F) and **one function out**. If the kinetics are underivable without the bounce's initial condition, the campaign files that honestly and the magnitude-and-form both join F17's invoice (jeopardy J-V1). Either outcome is a product.

**(2) The fitting hazard is the worst yet, so the protocol must be the strongest yet.** The current data lean somewhere ⬛-contested, and the sandbox is not amnesic. This pack therefore introduces **the sealed-adjudicator protocol (blind derivation):** the survey posteriors are named as an adjudicator class at charter and **sealed unquoted** (quarantine q-u — no numbers, no quadrant names from the data side appear in any derivation instrument); the derivation runs from corpus principles only, with its chain constructed to be **checkable as hint-independent**; a dedicated *unsealing instrument* (P4) performs the one comparison; and an **honesty clause** rides the whole campaign: *the protocol cannot erase the deriver's background knowledge — its value is that it forces the derivation chain to stand on cited corpus structure at every step, so the replication gate can test hint-independence in fact.* A landing in the data-flattered region under this protocol is reportable; a landing reached by any other road is not.

**(3) There is a real internal tension to audit, and it may be the campaign's catch.** The ⟨x6⟩ departure ledger bounds departures at δ_eq ∼ 10⁻³⁴; the dark-energy story says the not-yet-relaxed residual gravitates at **Ω_Λ ≈ 0.7 of critical.** Two ledger lines, thirty-four orders apart, both called "departures." Almost certainly the resolution is a **two-line structure** (the homogeneous cosmological residual vs. local/anomalous departures — different variables, different bounds), but *the ledger has never said so explicitly*, and a campaign that reconciles its own bookkeeping in public (clarification C-V1 or a seam, whichever the audit finds) is doing exactly what this archive is for.

**(4) The structural corollary is distinctive and cheap.** If the residual relaxes, **the acceleration is transient**: w → −1 from above and ρ_DE → 0 on the relaxation timescale — GUM's future is not eternal de Sitter. Untestable directly, but it shapes the w(z) tail, sharpens the divergence table (a new CA-1 row against the comparator's tracelessness-bounded Λ), and it is the kind of long-horizon sentence the corpus can own at structure grade for free.

**(5) The trinity meets here.** The residual's *initial condition* presses on **F17** (the magnitude joins the bounce's invoice — a fourth bill); its horizon-scale behavior brushes **F18**'s fence; and its non-clustering was the conscription-ban that protected **F19**'s mandate. The relaxation sector is where all three boundary markers touch one function — a consolidation bonus no other candidate offers.
