> **Primer margin · [beside the equilibrium passage] — "The strangest bet on the shelf."** For this theory, a confirmed violation of quantum mechanics — in the precise, narrow, non-equilibrium sense its own equations permit — would be simultaneously its greatest vindication and the end of its hiddenness: the same result that proves the guidance layer exists makes it visible forever after. Few theories own a result that would crown and expose them in one breath; fewer still print it in their own annex.
