This pack deliberately does **not** propose: any electroweak magnitude (walled at RK-2); any bounce-epoch simulation (R9); any 0νββ rate (S6's own adjudicators keep their job); or any η_B computation (F17 is the honest product). The campaign's center of gravity is P1–P3: **the ledger either extends or the theory has a blocking defect** — and per house rules, the sector is run expecting either verdict to be filed at full size. The queued sectors (WS-D, WS-T) inherit whichever universe survives.
