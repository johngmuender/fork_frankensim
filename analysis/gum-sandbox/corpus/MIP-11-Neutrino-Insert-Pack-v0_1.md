> **Primer, same chapter · [a second note] — "How to invoke an escape clause honestly."** Four rules, now case law: **(1)** the clause must be *pre-bound* — written and hashed before anyone knew which way it cuts, and you prove it by quoting; **(2)** its trigger must be met *by the world's own record* — you prove it by citing, and your own wishes are inadmissible; **(3)** the invocation *narrows* the claim — you pay less and risk the same, and the sentence saying so prints at full size; **(4)** the invocation is *trigger-maintained* — the moment the world's preference lapses, so does your clause, and you pre-commit the unwind. A clause invoked this way is integrity machinery working. A clause invoked any other way is a dodge with paperwork.
>
> **Primer margin · [beside the register] — "Two stakes, one sky, one seal."** The archive now holds two forward bets living in the same cosmological parameter space — one about dark energy, one about neutrino masses — and the worst thing it could ever do is let them vouch for each other.
