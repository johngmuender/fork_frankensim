# PART A — SECTOR AUDIT: THE QUESTION THE CORPUS NEVER ASKED ITSELF

## A.1 The situation

GUM is a **real-medium ontology**: the vacuum is a physical chiral Cosserat solid in three-dimensional space; particles are its knots and textures; the corpus's deepest chapters claim that **quantum mechanics itself emerges** from this medium's dynamics. Six campaigns have run *on top of* that claim without ever auditing it — every theorem from 𝔤_s = 2 to SC-V1 used quantum mechanics as a working framework and the substrate as its content. Meanwhile the outside world settled the one fact that every real-medium ontology must eventually face: **Bell's theorem plus the loophole-free experiments** ⬛. No theory whose complete state λ is local in 3-space, whose dynamics respect the emergent light cone, and whose measurement settings are independent of λ can reproduce the measured correlations. This is not a lore-extrapolation (F18's species) and not a missing sector (F19's); it is a **structural theorem with data attached**, aimed directly at the corpus's foundational chapters. The archive's "no questions of principle" declaration has survived six campaigns by growing honest caveats. **This campaign is its first direct test.**

## A.2 The five findings

**(1) The trilemma is exhaustive, and each horn has a price the archive can compute from its own shelves.** Any real-medium story meets Bell on exactly one of three horns: **(a) classical-local** — the substrate is a classical medium with 3-space-local dynamics → **refuted by ⬛ outright** (the only horn data kills without further argument); **(b) classical-nonlocal-hidden** — the substrate carries faster-than-cone influences at the hidden level that cannot be used to signal → collides with **the archive's own theorems**: WS-T's inertial silence (no preferred frame; the merged cone; δ ≤ 10⁻¹⁹) was derived *for the substrate's excitations*, and a hidden signaling layer must explain why it exists, why it hides, and why it leaves no fingerprint the ⟨x6⟩ ledger would have caught — a mechanism debt of the largest class, plus the standing conspiracy-adjacency of measurement-dependence escapes; **(c) already-quantum** — the substrate is a quantum medium, entanglement is native to its state space, Bell is satisfied the ordinary way → **survivable at the cost of the corpus's deepest ambition**: "the emergence of quantum mechanics" deflates to "the emergence of the Standard Model, the constants, and spacetime phenomenology *within* quantum mechanics." Expected product: **Theorem B-0 (the trilemma)**, with the exhaustiveness burden carried explicitly (jeopardy J-B3: a missed fourth horn — retrocausal and many-worlds readings must be located inside the trilemma or added to it, and the corpus's **one-world** commitment prices the many-worlds exit in advance).

**(2) The corpus must be read, not assumed — and the reading may find ambiguity, which is itself the finding.** The foundational chapters were written before this audit forced the question. P2 therefore performs two things no campaign has done: **a verbatim reading** (what do the chapters actually claim about the substrate's classicality?) and **the circularity audit** — the ħ-emergence chain walked step by step: *does the derivation of quantum structure from the medium anywhere borrow a quantum postulate?* (Quantization of circulation, the clock f = 1.236×10²⁰ Hz, the Born-rule and one-world passages — each step tagged: derived-from-classical-dynamics, or smuggled.) Candidate finding **F-B2-1 ("the unasked question")**: the corpus equivocates between horns because it never chose — and an equivocation, once named, must be resolved by adjudication, not by poetry.

**(3) The likely disposition is the archive's first grade-demotion event — and the severance tool already exists.** If P2 lands where A.2(2) suspects (quantum structure borrowed at load-bearing steps), the honest disposition is **horn (c) with the severance executed**: the corpus's claims split into **the surviving estate** — everything the six campaigns actually proved, none of which required QM to be emergent (checked in advance: WS-A through WS-V used substrate-plus-QM, never substrate-instead-of-QM) — and **the demoted ambition**: the emergence-of-QM-itself passages re-graded to aspiration class by *annotation* (S-30-compatible: nothing rewritten; a re-grade note affixed). The mercy is printable in one sentence: **the amputation costs the corpus its deepest ambition and none of its results.** And the residue gets a name — a candidate **fourth boundary marker, F20 ("the foundations flag")**: *what the substrate's state space must be* — the mandated structural requirement that any future completion supply the nonseparable correlation structure Bell demands, filed with two readings on the F19 pattern (soft: a derivation debt — the medium's configuration space shown rich enough; hard: the classical-local reading — already refuted, closed at filing).

**(4) The perimeter is real and mostly inherited.** Contextuality (Kochen–Specker class) rides the same disposition as Bell; **no-signaling** compliance is automatic on horn (c) and was already theorem-guarded (inertial silence) at the excitation level; the **Born-rule and one-world passages** get re-graded consistently with the main disposition (under horn (c), the measurement problem returns to the corpus in exactly the form it has in standard QM — the corpus may keep its one-world *preference* at interpretation grade and loses any claim to have *derived* it, unless P2's reading finds a derivation that survives the circularity audit); and the watch gains the **cosmic-Bell program class** ⬛ (setting choices sourced from high-redshift light — the experiments that squeeze measurement-dependence escapes), which prices horn (b)'s conspiracy corner ever downward without this archive lifting a finger.

**(5) The danger is the point, and the cage must protect the audit from its own rescuer instincts.** Three seductions are foreseeable and must be sealed at charter: **the web seduction** (the disclination network physically connects distant regions — the temptation to declare it a nonlocality mechanism *by assertion*; a real mechanism claim would need dynamics, a no-signaling proof, and a reconciliation with inertial silence — none of which exists, and the seal converts the temptation into a named derivation debt rather than a defense); **the bounce seduction** (superdeterminism via bounce-frozen correlations — measurement dependence *by assertion*, conspiracy-class, sealed with the cosmic-Bell pricing attached); and **the poetry seduction** (equivocating language — "the quantum is the medium's song" — that lets the corpus occupy horn (c) while sounding like horn (a); a language-lock, enforced by tariff).

## A.3 Jeopardies

**J-B1 (circularity lands):** the ħ-chain borrows quantum postulates → horn (c) forced; the demotion event executes; the declaration takes its first structural caveat *about the corpus's own foundations*. **J-B2 (the internal kill):** the reading finds an explicit, load-bearing classical-local claim → horn (a) → **the archive's second fired kill, and its first internal one** — a corpus *chapter* dies on ⬛; the branch is priced at charter with its own disposition script. **J-B3 (exhaustiveness):** the trilemma misses a horn → Theorem B-0 falls; the exhaustiveness proof is therefore the theorem's load-bearing clause and the replication gate's first target.
