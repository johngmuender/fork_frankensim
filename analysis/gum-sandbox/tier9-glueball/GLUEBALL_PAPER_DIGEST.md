## 1. The headline measurement (new in this Letter)

- Sample: **10 billion J/ψ events** ((10087 ± 44)×10⁶) at BESIII.
- Search: **X(2370) → K*(892)⁰K̄⁰ + c.c.** via J/ψ → γK_S⁰K_S⁰π⁰.
- Selection: ≥ 1 K_S⁰π⁰ combination in the K*(892)⁰ window
  (|m_{K_S⁰π⁰} − m_{K*(892)⁰}| ≤ 50 MeV/c²).  In the resulting
  K_S⁰K_S⁰π⁰ spectrum: clear peaks at 1.4 and 1.9 GeV/c², **no evident
  X(2370) peak** (their Fig. 1(d)).
- Fit: unbinned ML; X(2370) = efficiency-corrected PHSP-weighted
  Breit–Wigner ⊗ Gaussian resolution; mass/width **fixed to the combined
  2359 MeV/c² and 170 MeV** [their ref. 28 = arXiv:2605.26495]; 3rd-order
  polynomial background.  Statistical significance of X(2370):
  **0.1σ**.  𝓑_{K*K̄} ≡ 𝓑[J/ψ→γX(2370)]×𝓑[X→K*(892)⁰K̄⁰+c.c.→K_S⁰K_S⁰π⁰]
  = (0.1 ± 1.2(stat) ± 1.1(syst))×10⁻⁶.
- **Results:** 𝓑_{K*K̄} < 2.7×10⁻⁶ at 90% CL; ratio
  R = 𝓑[X→K*(892)⁰K̄⁰+c.c.→K_S⁰K_S⁰π⁰]/𝓑[X→K_S⁰K_S⁰π⁰]
  = 0.003 ± 0.040(stat) ± 0.026(syst), **R < 0.081 at 90% CL**.
- Systematics: X(2370) parametrization, PHSP parametrization, background
  model, possible additional resonances; efficiency uncertainty added for
  𝓑_{K*K̄}.

## 2. Why K*(892)K̄ suppression is the flavor-singlet signature

A **0⁻⁺ flavor-singlet meson is forbidden to decay to K*(892)K̄ by
generalized G-parity conservation** (Lipkin, PLB 106, 114 (1981); PLB 109,
326 (1982); Klempt–Zaitsev, Phys. Rept. 454, 1 (2007)).  Ordinary flavored
or flavor-octet pseudoscalars in this mass range decay to K*K̄ copiously
(η(1405)/η(1475): K*(892)K̄ is one of the major modes).  Hence the null
result ⇒ **X(2370) is a flavor-singlet — the first flavor-singlet light
hadron observed above 1 GeV/c²**.

## 4. The seven-criteria case for a dominant 0⁻⁺ glueball component

1. **Mass:** LQCD lightest 0⁻⁺ glueball 2.3–3.0 GeV/c² [refs 1–6: UKQCD
   1993; Morningstar–Peardon 1999; Y. Chen et al. 2006; Gregory et al.
   2012; Gui et al. 2019 (2.395 GeV); Vadacchino LATTICE2022 review].
   X(2370) sits inside the window.
2. **Spin-parity:** 0⁻⁺ measured at >9.8σ.
3. **Production:** LQCD 𝓑[J/ψ → γG_{0⁻⁺}] = (2.31 ± 0.90)×10⁻⁴ [5];
   glueball–cc̄ mixing (LQCD-predicted [10: Zhang et al. PLB 827, 136960
   (2022)]) with tiny mixing angle 2°–5° raises it to (2–20)×10⁻³
   [11: Y. Chen, L.-C. Gui, G. Li, W. Sun 2026, arXiv:2605.01757].
   Measured > 1×10⁻³ ✓.  KEY STRUCTURAL POINT: the mixing mechanism means
   the 0⁻⁺ glueball can ONLY be found as the glueball-dominant member of a
   glueball–cc̄ mixture; its decays remain glueball-like because both the
   glueball and η_c decay via gluons with the same J^PC.
4. **Decay-pattern similarity to η_c** (same J^PC, both gluon-mediated,
   both OZI-suppressed with no dominant single mode): observed modes
   KK̄π, ππη, ππη′, KK̄η′, a₀(980)π⁰ mirror η_c patterns.
5. **Flavor-singlet:** this Letter's K*(892)K̄ null (Sec. 2).
6. **Narrow partial widths:** flavor-symmetric S-wave quasi-two-body modes
   (f₀(980)η′, a₀(980)π⁰, K*₀(1430)K̄, f₀(1500)η, …) each at 𝓑 ~ 1–10% ⇒
   partial widths of a few MeV — far below typical OZI-allowed ~100 MeV.
   The **√OZI rule** [16: Robson, Nucl. Phys. B 130, 328 (1977)]:
   Γ(G_{0⁻⁺} → ππη) ≈ √(Γ_OZI-allowed × Γ(η_c → ππη))
   ~ √((100–200 MeV) × 0.5 MeV) ~ few MeV ✓ (decay to qq̄ OZI-suppressed
   at ONE vertex only — no initial qq̄ annihilation for a glueball).
7. **Radiative suppression to ω/φ:** γω and γφ tag uū+dd̄ and ss̄
   content [17–19]; both strongly suppressed for X(2370) (Table II)
   consistent with theoretical expectation for the pseudoscalar glueball
   [19: Hechenberger–Leutgeb–Rebhan, PRD 107, 114020 (2023)].

## 5. Interpretations disfavored (the Letter's adversarial part)

- **η–η′ radial excitation:** expected Γ(→K*(892)K̄) = 15–200 MeV
  [22: Yu–Sun–Liu–Zhao PRD 83, 114021 (2011); 38: Wang–Luo–Sun–Liu PRD 96,
  034013 (2017)]; naïve extrapolation from η(1405)/η(1475) gives >10 MeV,
  and P-wave PHSP at 2.37 GeV is 30× larger ⇒ >20 MeV.  Measured partial
  width < 2 MeV (from 𝓑[X→K*(892)K̄] < 1.6% × Γ = 170 MeV... as printed:
  "partial width of the X(2370) to K*(892)K̄ should be smaller than
  2 MeV").  INCONSISTENT.
- **qq̄ flavor-singlet:** LQCD predicts NO flavor-singlet state from
  uū+dd̄/ss̄ mixing near 2 GeV [39: Dudek et al. PRD 83, 111502 (2011)];
  experimentally no other flavor-singlet light meson above 1 GeV; γω/γφ
  suppressions say both light-quark contents are very small.
- **Multiquark / hybrid:** light-quark content ⇒ OZI-allowed dominated ⇒
  partial widths ~100 MeV expected; measured few-MeV widths disfavor
  [21–25 survey; 38].
- **ΣΣ̄ baryonium:** J/ψ radiative BFs to baryon pairs are ~10⁻⁴ or
  smaller (vs >10⁻³ needed); no line-shape distortion at the ΣΣ̄
  threshold; baryonium is not flavor-singlet.
- **Phenomenology tension noted:** [21: Sun–Dai–Kuang–Qin–Szczepaniak PRD
  105, 034010 (2022)] got 𝓑[J/ψ→γX] > 2.87×10⁻³ but with KK̄π/KK̄η′ > 50
  — inconsistent with Table I (ratio ≈ 8.3).  High production per se
  still favored by [10, 11, 37: Qin–Zhao–Zhong PRD 97, 096002 (2018)].
- **Honest caveats the Letter itself prints:** no single criterion
  unambiguously identifies a glueball; η_c-comparison limited by PHSP
  (η_c → f₀(1710)η′ dominant in KK̄η′ but no PHSP for X(2370) →
  f₀(1710)η′; multi-pion f₂(1270)f₂(1270) open for η_c, PHSP-suppressed
  for X); precise qq̄ fractions need partial-wave analyses incl.
  X(2120)/X(2260)/X(2600) interference and threshold effects; further
  singlet tests named: ωω, φφ enhanced, ωφ OZI-suppressed, K*(1410)K̄
  forbidden by generalized G-parity.
