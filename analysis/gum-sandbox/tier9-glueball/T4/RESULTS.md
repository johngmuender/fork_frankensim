## 4. T4-G2 — the priced alternative (symmetric honesty)

Two readings of Q-5, priced both ways.  **Neither is recommended; both
are priced.**  All mass statements referenced below are T1's, V.F-graded.

### 4.1 Option A — "Q-5 as printed (Skyrme-only)": the strong sector IS the Skyrme sector

**Costs (itemized):**

1. **The homelessness problem.**  The glueball-dominant X(2370)-class
   state has **no home**: the Skyrme spectrum contains no glueball-analog
   states (dictionary row 4's literature column), so if Q-5 exhausts the
   strong sector, the [IM]-identified state class — 0⁻⁺, flavor-singlet,
   2359 (+13/−14) MeV, seven-criteria case — has no in-model
   counterpart even at the structural level.  (Within-model cost only:
   nothing obliges the corpus to house any particular [IM] state; but see
   cost 2.)
2. **The corpus's own census strains.**  WS-D Theorem D-0's no-WIMP
   inventory is exhaustive and **presupposes** "stratum composites
   (glueball-analogs)" exist (WS-D:11) — the corpus's own relic census
   presupposes precisely the class the Skyrme-only identification cannot
   house.  Skyrme-only forces a choice: strike the census row (amending a
   dated record) or leave D-0's exhaustive inventory carrying a class the
   operative theory denies — an internal-consistency cost, not an
   external one.
3. **The "no new structure" advantage is partly illusory.**  Q-2 gives
   the T2 tube autonomous tension (01:615) and a knot-free closed loop is
   finite-energy by printed structure (tension × finite length; Q-1 binds
   only fractional windings).  Skyrme-only therefore needs a **new
   exclusion or instability theorem** — why the tube's closed loops do
   not appear in the spectrum (the only printed loop obituary is the
   T3-stratum web loop, WS-D:19 — the wrong stratum).  That theorem is
   itself new structure of the same order as what Option B pays.
4. **Course 20.1's "exactly" goes load-bearing.**  The completeness
   sentence "finite-energy states are exactly the line-neutral
   composites…" (02:1088) becomes the sector's exclusion clause while
   being, as printed, an unproven completeness claim (no compact-loop
   analysis exists anywhere to support the "exactly").

**Benefits:** no new sectors, no loop quantization, no F-Q8 discharge
owed (the state class is denied rather than owed closure — though at
cost 2's price); Q-5 stays verbatim; T-N5's monitoring scope stays
narrow.

### 4.2 Option B — "Q-5 + closed-string completion": the two-sector dictionary

**Costs (the new-structure bill, itemized):**

1. **Loop quantization machinery** [CJ]: compact-loop moduli, collective
   coordinates, J^PC operators — the printed h4/i4 monodromy apparatus
   covers properly-embedded lines only (h4_completion.md:63–99); a closed
   tube is an embedded S¹, outside every printed moduli space.
2. **The closure obligation must be discharged** (proposed F-Q8; T3 §3,
   adjudicated NEW-OPEN): exhibit the emergent-level ħ-clock for a
   knotless state, or print the excusal principle — [CJ-new] at every
   step; three branches, none printed.
3. **Worldsheet content beyond print**: as printed the tube is a plain
   bosonic string (T2 — two transverse Goldstones only), and T1 shows the
   axion-less spectrum misses the [IM] 0⁻⁺ window in both standard
   routes, in opposite directions (IP 8.27 high; NG 4.80 low and
   scalar-degenerate; ratio anchor 1.50 missed both sides) [V.F].
   Matching the phenomenology's shape requires the **tube-core axion**
   ([CJ-new]/[DW, testbed-grade], mass ∝ M_gap symbol-only) — and
   upstream of it, the locking-stratum mediator branch that is absent
   from the h25-audited field content (h25_RESULTS.md:53–59) must be
   exhibited at all.
4. **Sector-interface machinery** for the remaining scorecard rows:
   mixing (production, criterion 3 — GUM has no mixing machinery and no
   heavy-quarkonium sector to mix with), decay/width machinery (criteria
   4, 6 — none exists at any grade, WS-N:9), radiative transitions
   (criterion 7 — no vector-meson analogs).  Each [CJ]-class.
5. **Bookkeeping amendments**: the Course 20.1 "exactly" sentence needs a
   scope reading or amendment (it excludes the loop as written); the
   VII.J two-sector note; the WS-D cross-reference (annotations #1–#3).
6. **Even fully paid, the completion buys a home, not a number.**
   T1's finding stands inside Option B: the printed tension + standard
   quantization does not deliver the [IM] window; the axion-completed
   spectrum depends on M_gap, which is symbol-only in the entire archive.
   Everything quantitative remains at V.F — *dimensionally secure /
   structurally plausible / quantitatively unclaimed*.  The completion is
   necessary-not-sufficient for the mass row.

**Benefits:** houses the class D-0 presupposes (cost-2 symmetry: Option
B's benefit is exactly Option A's cost 2); matches the literature's own
two-description structure of real QCD (dictionary §2 reading); converts
the closure question from hidden to posable (F-Q8 becomes an open problem
instead of an unexamined presupposition); gives the flavor-singlet
criterion's structural support (T3 row 5) a state to attach to.

## 7. Honest caveats

1. **Memory-cited literature (the ° flags).**  The Skyrme-side references
   (Skyrme 1961; Witten 1979 ×2; Veneziano 1979; Adkins–Nappi–Witten
   1983; Hajduk–Schwesinger 1984; Nadkarni–Nielsen–Zahed 1985;
   Gomm–Jain–Johnson–Schechter 1986; Zahed–Brown 1986; Braaten–Carson
   1988; Barnes–Close–Swanson 1995; Leese–Manton–Schroers 1995;
   Amsler–Close 1996) are standard-knowledge citations from memory:
   the Tier-9 context sweep verified the glueball/flux-tube literature by
   snippet but did not sweep the Skyrme literature, and direct
   full-text verification is egress-blocked in this environment.  The
   *claims* they support (Skyrme spectrum = solitons + chiral quanta, no
   glueballs without an added field; breathing mode = baryon resonance;
   η′ anomaly-fed; B = 2 torus) are textbook-standard; exact bibliographic
   details carry the ° caveat.  Snippet-verified items (Isgur–Paton;
   ABT 1007.4720; Dubovsky–Hernández-Chifflet 1611.09796; PLB 827;
   PRD 107, 094510; PRL 110, 021601) are marked in-table.
2. **The dictionary is a proposal, not a discovery.**  "STRING sector"
   names a structure the corpus implies (Q-2's autonomous tension) but
   never constructs; assigning the glueball-analog class to it is the
   *only* consistent assignment found, not a printed fact.  The
   force-of-necessity argument (§2 reading) is structural, and its
   premises are the archaeology's negative sweeps — only as good as
   their grep coverage (same caveat as T3 §7.3).
3. **The homelessness cost is within-model only.**  Nothing obliges the
   corpus to house any [IM] state; Option A's cost 1 becomes binding
   pressure only through cost 2 (the corpus's own census) — which is why
   the census cross-reference (annotation #3) is the load-bearing offer,
   not the phenomenology.
4. **The pricing inherits T1/T2's own caveats** — in particular T1's
   IP-prescription reconstruction (amendments A1–A3) and scheme envelope,
   and T2's [CJ-new] ansatz flags.  Nothing in §4 sharpens their numbers;
   every mass statement here is a citation of theirs at V.F.
5. **Audit rows A/B/D resolve *potential* collisions.**  The clean
   verdict ("no actual double count") is cheap precisely because the
   corpus prints so little; if either sector's spectrum is ever
   constructed, the audit must be re-run against real states (the mixing
   bookkeeping in rows A/B is the template that will be needed).
6. **The optional annotation #4 is deliberately the weakest offer.**
   A monitoring row that cannot fire is close to content-free by design;
   it is included because the spec asks for the option, and because T-N5's
   existing scope (spectrum contradictions) does not cover completeness
   holes.  Declining it costs nothing.
7. **Forbidden-sentence compliance:** this workstream does not say and
   does not imply that the corpus predicted, is confirmed by, or is
   refuted by the [IM] anchor; no stake is issued; no grade moves; the
   𝔠 = 2.37 digit coincidence is cited nowhere as structure.  The
   self-scan in `t4_build.py` enforces this mechanically over all three
