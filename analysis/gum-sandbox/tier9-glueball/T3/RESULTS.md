### 3.2 The three resolution branches (pre-registered)

1. **Emergent-level closure mechanism for a knotless state.**  Exhibit the
   loop's ħ-clock.  Q-6′'s blanket sentence asserts line-neutral
   composites "close at the emergent level," but every *exhibited*
   mechanism runs through knot rotors (T-B1's w·j(1−j) = 1) or knot
   constituents (the B=2 composite, 01:831 App F.5).  A knotless loop has
   no constituents to aggregate; the natural candidate clock is the loop's
   own collective spectrum (exactly T1's rotational/vibrational modes),
   but no printed statement extends the closure condition to loop
   collective coordinates.  New construction, [CJ-new] at every step.
2. **Principled excusal.**  Print a principle under which the loop does
   not owe closure — e.g. only *stable* asymptotic states owe it (the
   census itself asserts the class decays, WS-D:11), exempting a
   finite-width resonance.  No such principle is printed.  The corpus's
   genre precedent for a *legal* evasal exists (the heliknoton "evading
   the mass floor legally, because its stabilizer is the background
   helix" — 01:831 App F.6), but no closure analog of it is printed.
3. **Cannot-close ⇒ contradiction with the relic census.**  If the loop
   provably cannot close at the emergent level, Q-6′ + T-B1 forbid the
   asymptotic state — but WS-D's relic census **presupposes the class
   exists** ("stratum composites (glueball-analogs) → decay to
   hadrons/phasons ✓", WS-D:11): Theorem D-0's no-WIMP inventory is
   exhaustive and needs the class in it.  An internal contradiction
