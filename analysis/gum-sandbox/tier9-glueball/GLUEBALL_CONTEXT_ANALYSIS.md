## 4. The structural confrontation (the analysis)

**4.1 The two-sector tension.**  GUM's printed long-wavelength
identification (Q-5) is *the hadronic Skyrme model* — whose spectrum
famously contains **no glueball states** (Skyrmions are baryons; the
orientation field's quanta are pion-like).  If Q-5 exhausted GUM's strong
sector, the corpus would have NO home for the now-identified
glueball-dominant X(2370).  It does not exhaust it: the **T2 tube itself**
is a separate printed structure with autonomous tension, and a knot-free
closed tube is exactly the Isgur–Paton/closed-string glueball object.  So
the honest within-model reading is a **two-sector dictionary**: knot
composites → emergent Skyrme (printed); pure-tube states → closed-string
sector (implied by Q-2, never constructed).  The literature's own history
supports the split: flux-tube models and Skyrme models are complementary
effective descriptions in real QCD too.

**4.2 The chirality hook (the genuinely novel within-model question).**
The 4D closed-string glueball spectrum *requires* a massive pseudoscalar
worldsheet mode (the axion) — and GUM's substrate is chiral by
constitutive choice (χ₁, χ₂, χ₃ pseudoscalar couplings; parity is not a
symmetry of the theory).  The corpus prints only *suppression* theorems
for this chirality (photon optical activity quenched; knot-matter
achirality gate χ* = 0) and is SILENT on the T2 tube core.  Whether the
printed chiral couplings induce a parity-odd worldsheet mode on the
mismatch tube — GUM's candidate for the worldsheet axion — is a
well-posed, executable question (T2 workstream), and its answer cuts both
ways: a natural pseudoscalar mode would be a structural accommodation of
the 0⁻⁺-glueball-first phenomenology; its absence would leave GUM's
closed-tube sector a plain bosonic string, inheriting the known 3+1D
Isgur–Paton J^PC problems.

**4.3 The flavor-singlet criterion is the one GUM passes trivially — and
the J^PC criterion is the one it cannot currently address.**  A knot-free
loop has no knot content, hence no flavor labels: flavor-singlet by
construction ([DF-structural reading]).  But the corpus possesses no
G-parity analog, no decay selection rules, no J^PC machinery for compact
loops (the h4/i4 monodromy apparatus is built for properly-embedded
lines; a closed tube is an embedded S¹ — outside every printed moduli
space), and no decay-width machinery of any kind for composites.  Of
BESIII's seven criteria, the corpus as printed can speak to: mass (only
via the T1 computation, V.F-graded), flavor-singlet (structurally), and
nothing else without new construction.

**4.4 The closure obligation (new internal consistency question).**
Q-6′: only asymptotic states owe closure at the vacuum's ħ.  The
glueball-analog is asymptotic.  Either (i) it closes at the emergent
level like line-neutral knot composites (mechanism unspecified for a
knotless state), (ii) it is excused by some unprinted principle, or
(iii) it cannot close — which would *forbid* the state the relic census
presupposes, an internal contradiction.  This is F-Q2′-class: a named,
sharply-posed obligation the sector owes.
