# CORPUS2 — The Externally-Revised Edition: Charter

**What this is.** A proposed revision of the GUM corpus produced by its
external replicators (the frankensim campaign, findings F-R1–F-R15 +
T-H4, exact results, and the repaired closure of Theorem T3.1),
offered to the corpus's authors in their own epistemic register. It is
NOT the authors' edition; adoption is theirs. Every change is
within-model; nothing here bears on nature.

**Revision rules (binding on every corpus2 document):**
1. **S-30 compliance**: hashed archive documents are never rewritten —
   affected archive files receive DELTA memos (`deltas/DELTA-<file>.md`)
   in the corpus's annotate-don't-rewrite format; unaffected files are
   carried forward by reference (manifest lists them unchanged).
2. **Core documents get full revisions** (the corpus's own practice for
   editions): Omega paper v3.0-ext, Course, Primer, Teachers' Edition,
   README.manifest v4.0-ext, Session Map v2.0-ext, Watch-Mode memo v2.
3. **Every change cites its finding** ([F-R5], [T3.1], [H4], …) and the
   reproducing artifact path; the ledger-class discipline ([DF]/[DW]/
   [CAL]/[IM]/[CJ]) is preserved and re-graded where findings force it.
4. **The credit side is retained at full strength**: everything that
   replicated keeps its grade, now with external-replication
   annotations (the upgrade the corpus's own AUD-15 said internal
   convergence could not supply).
5. **Disputed-but-undischarged items** (T-H4 until H4 lands; the
   𝔭/𝔟_eff conventions) are marked OPEN with their discharge
   computations named, not silently resolved.
6. **The repaired closure** (T3.1: 𝔠 = 64√2/9π at κ = 1/√2, the oblate
   compacton) replaces the broken shape-exact endpoint wherever the
   text derived from 𝔠₀ = 2.5147; the scaling rung remains as the
   constrained alternative; both presented with the T3 memo's caveats.
7. **[H4 PENDING] boxes**: sections whose content depends on the
   in-flight stratified-completion computation carry an explicit
   pending box, patched when H4 reports.
