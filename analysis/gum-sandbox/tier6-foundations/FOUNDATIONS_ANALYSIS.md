## 2. GUM's foliation vs GRWm's past light cone: the architectural fork

The GRWm paper solves a problem GUM *does not have*, by a mechanism GUM
*cannot use* — and the comparison sharpens both.

**2.1 The problem GRWm solves.** With ontology = a functional of ψ, and ψ
hypersurface-dependent (ψ_Σ1 ≠ ψ_Σ2 on Σ₁∩Σ₂, even as reduced density
matrices — their Eq. (4)), the matter density m(x) inherits frame
dependence unless a covariant evaluation rule is chosen. Their law —
evaluate on PLC(x) (their Eq. (5)) — makes m(x) a function of x alone.
The price: ∫_Σ m d³x is not conserved (their §V), the law breaks
time-reversal (their §VII), and the FLC alternative is excluded only by
*no-micro-signalling* (their (10)) — i.e., by a locality-of-record
criterion, not by covariance itself.

**2.2 Why GUM does not have the problem.** GUM's primitive ontology is
not a functional of ψ. It is the substrate field (u, Q̃) and its knots —
local fields on spacetime whose values at x are hypersurface-independent
*trivially*, the way any classical field's are. The wave function of
Sec. III is coarse-grained bookkeeping of the medium (and the conditional
wave function of III.F is itself a physical medium field via the Norsen
tower — local again). GRWm's central difficulty — "which ψ do I read
m(x) from?" — never arises. **This is a genuine architectural advantage
of medium-first over ψ-first ontologies, and the corpus never claims the
credit explicitly.** [Proposed annotation site: Sec. VIII.A, one
sentence + pointer here.]

**2.3 What GUM pays instead.** GRWm needs no foliation: covariance is
bought by PLC + an intrinsically stochastic ψ-law satisfying (A1)–(A5).
GUM needs the distinguished foliation F — materially supplied
(VI.E), hidden at equilibrium (QFT-1), but *real structure* — and pays
with foliation-relative jump micro-histories (QFT-1's honest remainder)
plus the standing debts WS-QFT-2 (all-orders spacelike commutativity)
and the Lock-2 leakage exponent (T4-W3). The fork, stated symmetrically:

| | GRWm (5) | GUM VIII/VIII′ |
|---|---|---|
| PO | m(x) from ψ on PLC(x) | substrate field + knot positions |
| Extra spacetime structure | none | foliation F (from substrate state) |
| Covariance of record statistics | (A4)–(A5) ⇒ their (11)–(12) | QFT-1 (equivariant-jump covariance) |
| Sub-record covariance | m(x) itself covariant; micro-randomness in ψ-law | micro-histories foliation-relative (exposed at A3 gate only) |
| No-signalling | no-micro-signalling (10), law-level, unconditional | Lock 1, **equilibrium-contingent** (the unlock VIII.E is a feature) |
| Determinism | stochastic ψ-law essential | deterministic guidance + stochastic nucleation jumps (8′.1) |
| Empirical departures from QM | in principle (collapse rate params) | in principle (non-equilibrium relics; A3; band-edge tier 3) |

| Time-reversal | broken by law for m *and* law for ψ | broken by relaxation (H-theorem) only; micro-law T-even |

**F-T6-3 (discriminator ledger, new).** The two architectures are

## 3. The Lorentz-invariance ledger, updated by the four texts

Bell's final formulation (Goldstein §1.9): *which, if either, of these
two precise pictures can be redeveloped in a Lorentz invariant way?*
Three post-Bell answers are now on record: flash-GRW (Tumulka 2006),
GRWm-on-PLC (2014, this paper), and covariant-foliation Bohmian
mechanics (Dürr–Goldstein–Norsen–Struyve–Zanghì 2013: extract F from ψ
covariantly). GUM's position in this taxonomy is distinctive and should
be printed as such:

- DGNSZ 2013 extract F **from ψ** (covariant law, no extra ontology,
  but F is nomological superstructure);
- GUM extracts F **from matter** (the substrate rest slicing — the same
  move that makes the CMB frame unobjectionable), making the foliation
  law-like *and* contingent, and — uniquely in this family —
  **empirically exposed** (Layer 2's sidereal probe, IX.C, with the
  Machian-dragged v_F prior).

**F-T6-4 (positioning, credit).** GUM is the only member of the
foliation family whose foliation is (a) sourced by physical degrees of
freedom that gravitate and drag, and (b) attached to a scheduled
detection program with a pre-registered five-branch decision tree. The
corpus should cite DGNSZ 2013 as the nearest structural relative and
state the two deltas above; at present Sec. VI.E/VIII.B carry no
comparison anchor. [Documentation-grade; no regrade.]

**The equilibrium hinge, made explicit.** Both GUM locks (Valentini
no-signalling; Lock 2 silence) are equilibrium-contingent, where GRWm's
(10) is law-level. This is GUM's deliberate trade — the unlock is a
prediction surface — but it obligates the corpus to the missing
conservation-law lemma behind Lock 2's (c/c_L)⁵ (T4-W3 names the debt:
the exponent presupposes vanishing monopole and dipole channels of the

conserved → 5) is the toy-grade discharge.

## 4. The A3 gate, re-read through Bell's morals

Bell 1982 bans "measurement" in favor of "experiment"; the corpus's own
T4-W5 repair (enumeration over proposals ≠ exclusion over the POVM
class) is the same discipline applied to GUM's most exposed claim. The
four texts sharpen the gate's logic:

1. Bricmont's ToF example shows a "momentum measurement" that is a
   position experiment whose statistic happens to be |Ψ̂|² — a POVM.
   The A3 claim is that the *transverse-spin arrival cutoff* is a
   position-experiment statistic that is **not** any POVM on H_B.

   between "orthodox proposals fail" and "Layer 2 is licensed."
2. GRWm's (11)–(12) show that *any* (A4)/(A5)-collapse theory's records
   are POVM-representable — so A3-positive would falsify the whole
   collapse family, not just orthodox arrival proposals. The gate is
   sharper than the corpus prints: it discriminates GUM not only from
   orthodox QM but from GRW/CSL/GRWm as a class. [Proposed annotation:
   VIII.F/IX.B, one sentence.]
3. Workstream **L5** (this tier) reproduces the phenomenon itself on an
   independent engine — 2-D Pauli-guidance waveguide, transverse vs
   axial spin, hard support cutoff vs its absence, far-field null — the
   first in-repo instantiation of the Layer-1 envelope's core physics
   (X-Vb1-adjacent), and the concrete testbed for any future
   POVM-exclusion argument.
